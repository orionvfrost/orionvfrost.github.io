About me

Hi, I am Dhaana. I am a computer science student at UTM specialising in data engineering. I like the part of computing where messy real-world data becomes something people can trust and use.

Outside class I build small systems end to end: a food stall order app that turns WhatsApp messages into database records, a gym management tool for a friend, and an AWS data pipeline project. I also practise security skills on Hack The Box Academy, because I want data systems I build to be safe by design.

My long-term goal is to build a security product for operational technology and industrial control systems in Southeast Asia.
