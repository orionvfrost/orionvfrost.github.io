About me

Hi, I am Dhaana. I am a computer science student at UTM specialising in data engineering. I like the part of computing where messy real-world data becomes something people can trust and use.

My first real project is a food stall order app. Customers send orders as ordinary WhatsApp-style messages, an AI model turns them into structured data, and a database and dashboard show the stall owner what is selling. I worked on the database and analytics side of it, and it taught me more about data engineering than any single lecture.

I want to keep building systems like this: simple inputs on one end, reliable and well-organised data on the other.

A four-person team project. A customer sends an order in a WhatsApp-style message, Google Gemini parses it into JSON, the order is saved to a database, and a dashboard shows sales.

How the data flows

My contribution

I was Developer 2 and owned the database and analytics. I designed the schema in schema.sql with three tables (items, orders and order_items), wrote seed_data.py to fill it with sample data, and wrote analytics.py to summarise orders with Pandas for the dashboard.

Reflection

What I learned
Splitting orders from order items taught me why a database is designed around its data, not just filled in. One order can hold many items, so keeping them in separate linked tables avoids repeating data and makes questions like "which item sells most?" easy to answer with Pandas. I also saw how the stages of a pipeline depend on each other: the dashboard is only as good as the records the database receives.
